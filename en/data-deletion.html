Data deletion

Revoke access on the platform

If you authorized an integration, open the platform’s security or connected app settings and remove Vice City Studio if it is listed. This ends the access granted by the platform.

Request deletion by email

Use the button below. Specify the platform, the account identifier relevant to the request, and the data to delete. Keep passwords, tokens, authentication codes, and identity documents out of the initial message.

How requests are handled

The studio reviews the request, may confirm account ownership, and responds by email after processing it. Deletion covers data under the studio’s control, including relevant locally held credentials where applicable, subject to legal retention obligations. For data held by a third-party platform, contact that platform directly.
