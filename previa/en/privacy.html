Privacy policy

Website and contact data

This static site provides no sign-up, contact form, or studio-run audience analytics. GitHub Pages may process technical data such as IP addresses to host and protect the service. When emailing, you provide your address, message, and any information you choose to include. The studio uses this data to respond, handle requests, and meet applicable obligations.

External APIs and Gemini Free Tier

The studio uses YouTube for queries and the Gemini Free Tier for production support. Each provider handles data submitted to its API under its own terms. On the Gemini Free Tier, Google may use inputs and outputs to improve services and human reviewers may review them, subject to applicable terms. Privacy requests, credentials, and other personal or confidential data should stay out of Gemini.

OAuth and local credentials

This site does not initiate OAuth or store passwords or tokens in your browser. When an integration requires OAuth, credentials and tokens are stored in the studio’s local production environment, outside the website and public repository. Access is limited to granted scopes. TikTok and Instagram are in development and authorization is unavailable on this site.

Retention and sharing

Messages and operational data should be retained as needed for the stated purposes and applicable obligations. Hosting, email, and APIs involve providers with their own policies. Social links lead to external services that may process data under their own policies.

Revocation, deletion, and rights

You can revoke permissions in the connected platform’s account settings and ask the studio to delete data it holds. To request access, correction, deletion, or exercise other applicable rights, email the privacy contact. The studio may ask for proportionate information to verify your identity.
